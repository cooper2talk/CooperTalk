Cooper2Talk Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Cooper2Talk provides an AI-assisted telephone receptionist and operator console for Cooper Talk. This policy explains how the service handles information from calls and WhatsApp communications.

Information we process

We may process caller phone numbers, call time and duration, call-routing metadata, text transcripts, operator instructions, and WhatsApp message delivery status. We do not record or store call audio.

How we use information

We use this information to answer and route calls, provide the operator console, create call summaries, send authorised WhatsApp updates, maintain service security, and investigate delivery or technical issues.

Service providers

Telephone, transcription, AI, voice synthesis, and WhatsApp functions may be provided by Telnyx, Deepgram, Groq, Google Cloud, and Meta. These providers process information only as needed to provide their respective services.

Retention

Call transcripts are retained for up to 30 days, then deleted according to the service retention process. Limited call, audit, and delivery metadata may be retained for security, operational, and compliance purposes.

Your choices and data requests

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information connected with a Cooper2Talk call or WhatsApp message, contact cooper2talk@gmail.com. Please include the phone number used, the approximate call or message date, and your request so we can locate the relevant record securely.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections, and restricted service credentials to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please avoid sharing highly sensitive information by phone unless necessary.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service changes. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.

For questions about this policy, contact cooper2talk@gmail.com.
